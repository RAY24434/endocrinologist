About the Doctor

Specialist care with a thoughtful, patient-centred approach.

Dr. Yakubu Lawal is a Consultant Endocrinologist dedicated to helping patients understand complex hormonal and metabolic conditions.

His practice combines evidence-based medicine, careful diagnosis and personalised treatment plans designed around each patient's circumstances, goals and long-term wellbeing.

He has a particular interest in diabetes care and management, thyroid disorders,endocrine disorders, obesity medicine and metabolic health.
